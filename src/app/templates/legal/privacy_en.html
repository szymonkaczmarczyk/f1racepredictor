Race Predictor shows predictions for Formula 1 races. It has no user accounts, sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or other tracking. This policy explains the small amount of data that is processed when you visit it.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is {{ legal.operator }}, {{ legal.address }}. For anything related to privacy, write to {{ legal.email }}.

What is processed and why

  • Server logs. When your browser loads a page, the server and our hosting provider record technical data: IP address, date and time, the address requested, the response status, the browser's user agent and the referring page. We use them only to run the service, keep it secure and investigate faults and attacks. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are deleted after {{ legal.log_days }} days, unless a specific incident requires keeping part of them for longer.
  • Predictions you run. The starting grid and conditions you choose are sent to the server to calculate the prediction and are not stored. The predictions the app records before each race (the track record) concern drivers and races and contain no information about you.
  • Settings in your browser. Your language and colour theme are saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers them. They never leave your device, are not cookies and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings.
  • Sharing a prediction. The podium image is created in your browser. If you share it, it goes to the app you choose on your device; we receive nothing.

No third parties in your browser

All pages, scripts, fonts and images are served from our own server. Your browser does not contact Google, social networks or advertising networks when you use Race Predictor. The links to data sources in the footer lead to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Race data and weather forecasts are fetched by our server from public sources (Jolpica-F1, OpenF1, FastF1 and Open-Meteo). No information about visitors is sent to them.

Hosting

The service runs on servers provided by {{ legal.hosting }}, which processes the server logs on our behalf under a data processing agreement and only as needed to provide the hosting.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work. As we do not know who you are, we can usually find log entries about you only if you give us the exact time of your visit and your IP address. To exercise your rights, write to {{ legal.email }}.
